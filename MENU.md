# 🍳 Team Cooking Club — Kickoff Week Menu

We're kicking off the cooking club: **5 of us**, one shared lunch every weekday, and a different cook each day.

**Dietary rules for the whole plan (double-checked on every recipe below):**

- 🚫 **No shrimp (虾)** — a teammate is allergic
- 🚫 **No cilantro (香菜)** — the organizer can't stand it

The week was built from a personalized meal recommendation generated for our group of 5 with **shrimp and cilantro excluded**. The two mains below are the best-looking picks from that recommended plan (both come from **Monday / 周一** of the plan), the two sides were chosen from the 素菜 (vegetarian) category, and Day 1 breakfast is the team's fixed must-have.
