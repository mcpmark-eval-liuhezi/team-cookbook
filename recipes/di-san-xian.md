# 地三鲜 (Di San Xian — Pan-Fried Potato, Eggplant & Green Pepper)

> **Vegetarian side #1**, chosen from the 素菜 (vegetarian dishes) category.
>
> **Category:** 素菜 · **Difficulty:** ★★★
>
> The classic northeastern Chinese trio — potato, eggplant and green pepper — fried until golden, then tossed in a savoury, lightly sweet sauce. A guaranteed rice-killer that rounds out the meat and fish mains.

**Team note:** base recipe below; multiply by 5 for the whole team (see [shopping-list.md](../shopping-list.md)).

**Allergy check:** fully vegetarian; **no shrimp, no cilantro.**

## Ingredients

- 茄子 100 g (eggplant; ~500 g for the team)
- 土豆 150 g (potato; ~750 g for the team)
- 尖椒 3–4 个 (green chili peppers; ~15–20 for the team)
- 葱 3 g (scallion)
- 姜 10 g (ginger)
- 蒜 20 g (garlic, divided into two additions)
- 豆瓣酱 20 ml (bean paste)
- 生抽 10 ml (light soy sauce)
- 盐 8 g (salt)
- 糖 10 g (sugar)
- 淀粉 20 g (starch)
- 食用油 25 ml, plus up to 15 ml more if needed
- 水 200 ml (water)

## Steps

1. Wash and peel the potatoes. Rinse the eggplant and green peppers.
2. Cut 3 g of scallion into 0.5 cm pieces. Mince 10 g of garlic. Finely chop 10 g of ginger.
3. Cut the eggplant, potato and green peppers into roughly 15 g pieces each.
4. Heat the pan, then add 25 ml oil.
5. Add the potato and fry for about 3 minutes, until about 80% cooked and golden.
6. Remove the potatoes, leaving the oil in the pan.
7. Add the eggplant and fry for about 40 seconds, until about 70% cooked and golden.
8. If no free-flowing oil is left in the pan, add another 15 ml.
9. Add 3 g scallion and 10 g ginger.
10. Add 20 ml 豆瓣酱.
11. Add 10 ml 生抽.
12. Add 8 g salt.
13. Add 10 g sugar.
14. Return the fried potatoes to the pan.
15. Add the green peppers.
16. Stir-fry for 1 minute.
17. Add the remaining 10 g of garlic.
18. Add 200 ml water mixed with 20 g starch.
19. Once the sauce boils and reduces by about half, turn off the heat and plate.
