# 手抓饼 (Shou Zhua Bing — Hand-Torn Flaky Pancake with Egg, Ham, Lettuce & Cheese)

> **Day 1 breakfast — the team's fixed must-have.**
>
> **Category:** 早餐 (breakfast) · **Difficulty:** ★★ · Serves 1 per batch (make 5 for the team).
>
> A street-food classic: a many-layered, crispy-on-the-outside, chewy-on-the-inside pancake wrapped around a fried egg, ham, fresh lettuce and a slice of cheese. The perfect start to the kickoff day.

**Allergy check:** contains wheat, egg, ham (pork) and dairy; **no shrimp, no cilantro.**

## Ingredients (per serving — make 5 for the team)

**For the dough**
- 普通面粉 200 g (all-purpose flour)
- 开水 100 ml (boiling water)
- 冷水 50 ml (cold water)
- 食用油 15 ml (cooking oil, for layering and frying)
- 盐 3 g (salt)

**For the filling**
- 鸡蛋 1 个 (egg)
- 生菜 30 g (lettuce)
- 火腿 30 g (ham)
- 芝士片 1 片 (cheese slice)

> The ingredient list and quantities above are taken verbatim from the source recipe; its written step section did not parse cleanly, so the steps below follow the standard preparation for this classic dish.

## Steps

1. **Make a hot-water dough:** sift the flour into a bowl with the salt, pour in the boiling water while stirring with chopsticks, then add the cold water. Knead into a smooth, soft, slightly tacky dough.
2. **Rest:** cover and let the dough rest for 20–30 minutes so it relaxes and rolls out easily.
3. **Build the layers:** divide the dough, roll one portion into a very thin rectangle, brush the surface with oil, then roll it up tightly into a rope.
4. **Coil:** curl the rope into a flat spiral, tuck the end underneath, and let it rest a few minutes.
5. **Roll out:** flatten the spiral and roll it into a thin pancake — the coil is what creates the signature flaky layers.
6. **Pan-fry:** heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat, cook the pancake until golden and puffed, flipping and pressing gently so every layer crisps up.
7. **Fry the egg:** in the same pan, fry the egg, then set the pancake on top of it for a few seconds so they stick together.
8. **Fill & wrap:** layer on the ham, lettuce and cheese slice, roll everything up, and serve hot while the outside is still crisp.
