# 水煮鱼 (Shui Zhu Yu — Fish in Spicy Chili Broth)

> **From the recommended plan: Monday (周一)** — selected as **Main #1** for the team's Day 1 lunch.
>
> **Category:** 水产 (aquatic) · **Difficulty:** ★★★★ · Beginners should allow about 2 hours.
>
> Basa is a lean, high-protein, low-fat fish, and it is poached with seasonal vegetables, so the dish is hearty but still on the healthy side. This is the crowd-pleasing centerpiece of the kickoff lunch.

**Team note:** the quantities below are the base recipe; for 5 people use about 1 kg of fish and roughly double the vegetables and seasonings (see [shopping-list.md](../shopping-list.md)).

**Allergy check:** contains fish; **no shrimp, no cilantro**. (The heat comes from 红油豆瓣酱 and 藤椒油, not shellfish.)

## Ingredients

**Main**
- 巴沙鱼 500 g (basa fillets; use ~1 kg for the team of 5)
- 蔬菜 300–500 g total — e.g. 花菜 300 g + 生菜 200 g (any mix of potato slices / bean sprouts / cauliflower / lettuce works)

**Seasonings**
- 红油豆瓣酱 40 g (add 10–20 g more if you like it spicier)
- 豆豉 10 g (optional)
- 藤椒油 10 ml
- 菜籽油 25 ml (plus ~20 ml for frying the paste)
- 白胡椒粉 3 g
- 大蒜 2 瓣 (plus extra, minced)
- 盐 5 g
- 糖 2 g

**Equipment**
- 量杯
- 厨房秤 (optional)
- 大不锈钢碗

## Steps

1. **Thaw:** if the basa came from the freezer, let it thaw at room temperature for about 5 hours before slicing.
2. **Slice:** cut the basa into thin slices about 5 cm long and 3 cm wide.
3. **Marinate:** put the sliced fish into a large stainless-steel bowl.
4. Add 30 g 豆瓣酱, 3 g salt, 10 ml 藤椒油 and 3 g 白胡椒粉.
5. Mix by hand, then finish with 5 ml 菜籽油 to seal in the flavour.
6. Let it rest at room temperature for at least 30 minutes to absorb the marinade.
7. **Prep the vegetables:** mince the garlic. Using 300 g cauliflower and 200 g lettuce as the example, wash both.
8. **Blanch & stir-fry:** blanch the cauliflower in a pot of boiling water and set aside; wash and dry the lettuce, then stir-fry it until just cooked (no oil needed) and set aside.
9. **Fry the paste:** hot pan with cold oil (20 ml 菜籽油), add 10 g 豆瓣酱 and 10 g 豆豉 (optional), then the minced garlic, and slow-fry over **medium heat**.
10. **Poach the fish:** pour in 150 ml hot water; once it comes back to a boil, add the marinated fish slices and gently stir so they separate. Season with 2 g salt and 2 g sugar (adjust the salt to taste). Once the water boils again, it is ready to plate.
11. **Plate:** put the cooked vegetables in a large bowl first, lay the hot fish slices on top of them, then pour over the remaining hot broth. Serve!
