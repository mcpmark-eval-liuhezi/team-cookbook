# 松仁玉米 (Song Ren Yu Mi — Sweet Corn with Toasted Pine Nuts)

> **Vegetarian side #2**, chosen from the 素菜 (vegetarian dishes) category.
>
> **Category:** 素菜 · **Difficulty:** ★★
>
> A colourful, sweet-and-fresh classic: tender sweet corn, crisp toasted pine nuts, and a glossy light sauce. Loved by everyone, kids included — the easy, bright counterpoint to the two rich mains.

**Team note:** base recipe below; multiply by 5 for the whole team (see [shopping-list.md](../shopping-list.md)).

**Allergy check:** contains pine nuts (tree nuts); **no shrimp, no cilantro.**

## Ingredients

- 玉米粒 200 g (sweet corn kernels — canned sweet corn works, or cook your own; ~1 kg for the team)
- 熟松子仁 30 g (roasted pine nuts; ~150 g for the team)
- 胡萝卜 50 g (carrot, diced small — optional, for colour)
- 食用油 15 ml (cooking oil)
- 白砂糖 10 g (sugar)
- 盐 1 g (salt)
- 淀粉 5 g (starch)
- 水 20 ml (for the starch slurry)

## Steps

1. Blanch the corn kernels and carrot dice for 1 minute, then drain well and set aside.
2. Heat the pan with cold oil, add the carrot dice and stir briefly, then add the corn kernels and stir-fry.
3. Add the sugar and salt, and stir until evenly seasoned.
4. Mix the water with the starch to make a slurry, pour it in and toss quickly so the sauce thickens slightly.
5. Add the roasted pine nuts and toss to combine.
6. Plate and serve.
