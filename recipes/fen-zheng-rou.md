# 粉蒸肉 (Fen Zheng Rou — Steamed Pork Belly in Rice Flour)

> **From the recommended plan: Monday (周一)** — selected as **Main #2** for the team's Day 1 lunch.
>
> **Category:** 荤菜 (meat dish) · **Difficulty:** ★★★★ · About 90 minutes from prep to table.
>
> A classic Chinese steamed dish: intensely aromatic, meltingly soft, and nourishing. Perfect for a family-style group meal or a festive table.

**Team note:** base recipe below; for 5 people use 750 g–1 kg of pork belly (see [shopping-list.md](../shopping-list.md)).

**Allergy check:** **no shrimp, no cilantro.**

## Ingredients

- 五花肉 500 g (pork belly, layered fat and lean; use 750 g–1 kg for the team)
- 蒸肉米粉 100 g (steamed-pork rice flour — 李锦记 brand or homemade)
- 生抽 15 ml
- 老抽 10 ml
- 料酒 15 ml
- 郫县豆瓣酱 10 g (optional)
- 姜末 10 g (minced ginger)
- 蒜末 10 g (minced garlic)
- 白砂糖 5 g
- 土豆 300 g (or 南瓜 300 g — potato or pumpkin, as the base layer)
- 清水 2000 ml (for the steamer)

## Steps

1. Rinse the pork belly and cut it into slices about 5 cm long, 3 cm wide and 0.5 cm thick.
2. Mince the ginger and garlic until the particles are no larger than 1 mm across.
3. In a large bowl, combine the sliced pork with 15 ml 生抽, 10 ml 老抽, 15 ml 料酒, 10 g 郫县豆瓣酱, 10 g 姜末, 10 g 蒜末 and 5 g 白砂糖.
4. Stir well with chopsticks, cover with cling film, and let it marinate at room temperature (20–25 °C) for 30 minutes.
5. After marinating, add 100 g 蒸肉米粉 and toss for another 2 minutes, making sure every slice is evenly coated.
6. Peel the potatoes and cut into 0.8 cm-thick slices roughly 5 cm × 5 cm, about 300 g in total.
7. Line the bottom of a 20 cm-wide deep bowl with the potato slices, avoiding overlap as much as possible.
8. Spread the coated pork belly evenly over the potatoes and press it down firmly.
9. Add 2000 ml water to the steamer and heat it until the water is steadily bubbling (100 °C).
10. Place the filled bowl into the steamer and put the lid on.
11. Steam over medium heat for 60 minutes (keep the heat at a steady, continuous boil, roughly 600 W).
12. When the time is up, poke a chopstick into the centre of the meat — if it goes through easily with no real resistance, the pork is done.
13. If it is not there yet, steam for another 10–15 minutes until the meat is fall-apart tender and the fat has rendered out.
14. Remove from the steamer, plate, and serve.
